Hi! We’re Lidia and Andrei, a couple who turned traveling and filmmaking into our lifestyle. For the past four years, we’ve been living abroad, exploring new places, and capturing them on camera. We film together, taking turns behind the lens and adding our own details to make every story unique.

Lidia brings the magic in post-production: editing, color grading, sound design, and music.

With her modeling background, she also knows how to shine in front of the camera.

Andrei is the diamond cut of our projects, the one who shapes ideas and polishes every detail until it shines. He sparks concepts, draws storyboards, and makes sure each video feels alive.

Together, we create videos that aren’t just about destinations,
they’re about the feeling of being there.

InterContinental. Da Nang Sun Peninsula Resort

InterContinental Da Nang is one of those rare places that speaks for itself. Nestled into the cliffs above the South China Sea, every corner of the resort carries a sense of scale and intention that is genuinely hard to find. Filming here felt less like work and more like exploring somewhere extraordinary. A truly unique property.

enjoi

Enjoi. Chang Rai China. Chongquing Japan Hong Kong

China in motion. Fog over mountains, neon reflections in rain-soaked streets — shot on Sony ZV-E1, cut together in Procreate Dreams.

Chiang Rai didn't feel real. Every turn looked staged — until you remembered it wasn't.

Japan doesn't shout its beauty. It's in the quiet — a shrine gate in the fog, neon reflected in wet asphalt, the way light falls through a paper screen.

Hong Kong, together. Neon canyons, night markets, a city that never quite lets you catch your breath — this is what our life looked like there.
